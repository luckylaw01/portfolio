Good! Now let's have a glimpse of the hierachy of students placement and teacher's placement. We have an institution. The instituions hosts diferent departments. Now there are several courses attached to each of these departments. These are the courses that students select to study when joining. For example the computing and Information Technology Department (CIT) may have Computer science (code CIT-211), computer Technology (CIT-222), Software Engineering (CIT-227), etc. Now obviuosly the courses have a collection of units they are expected to learn in the course of their study. Some units are done by multiple courses, infact, others are done by all of them. When a student chooses to study for example computer science they are allocated to a group of students based on their intake, this is what we refer to as a class. for example a class called Computer science septemmber 2025 intake.  This is where teachers come in. They are assigned to teach a certain class a certain unit, during a certain semester (May-July 2025 semester). Teachers Are also genrary categorized to belong to a department but they may teach other departments. Then we have blocks and rooms, Naturary blocks are also assigned to a certain department, even though, while the priority is given to the owning department, the rooms in these blocks can be used by other departments. the rooms could be categorized as classrooms, labs or workshops or other. There are common blocks and common rooms. So, when a teacher meets a certain group of students (class) in a certain room for a certain unit during a certain peiod, that is a session.
